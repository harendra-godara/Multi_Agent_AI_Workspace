# Assignment 3 — SOAP Integration
Multi-Agent AI Workspace uses an external SOAP validation partner for artifact validation.

Operation: validateArtifact
Input: workflowRunId, artifactRef
Output: verdict, score, comments
Fault: ValidationFault / partner unavailable

The AI Agent or Execution & Artifact service is the caller. The WSDL is the published contract. Internal data stores and provider credentials remain hidden. SOAP faults are mapped to clean application errors; partner outages are transient and may be retried with timeout/backoff.
